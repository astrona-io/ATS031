# Summary

Artifact verification connects the exact bytes selected for deployment to integrity and publisher evidence. A checksum detects a difference only when the expected digest is trusted, while a signature needs a justified signer identity.

Release, architecture and verification policy must agree. Store and deploy the verified artifact without allowing replacement, and assess vulnerabilities separately because authenticity does not establish absence of defects.
